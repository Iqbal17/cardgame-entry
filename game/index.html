Four elements. One hand. Outwit the rival.

You and the rival start at 30 life. Each turn you gain 3 ember and draw up to 5 cards. Play cards by clicking them — each costs ember. Two cards of the same element in a row spark a combo (+1). Reduce the rival to 0 life to win.

How to play

  • Goal: reduce the rival from 30 life to 0 before yours reaches 0.
  • Ember: you gain 3 ember each turn. Every card costs ember to play.
  • Suits: ▲ Flame hits hard, ● Tide heals and draws, ◆ Thorn shields and bites, ★ Spark draws and combos.
  • Combo: play two cards of the same element in a row and the second one gets +1 to its numbers.
  • Shield: absorbs damage first, then fades at the start of your next turn.
  • End turn when you are done — the rival plays, then you draw back up to 5 cards.
